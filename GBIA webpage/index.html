Our Mission

Grace Bible Integrated Academy endeavors to help young people develop and mature in a positive, Bible-centered environment that integrates faith and learning by emphasizing Christians ambassadors for Christ who will make lasting impact wherever they minister.

Grace Bible Integrated Academy is committed to providing a sustainable Bible-centered education integrated with holistic training in preparing culturally diverse and intelligent students leading to a life commitment and service to God and society.
